## What ApexLogger Does

ApexLogger gives drivers a wearable-centered method for recording telemetry during track days. The Apple Watch workflow keeps important driving information nearby without requiring the driver to manage a separate logging setup during a session.

The app is intended for auto enthusiasts who want to capture runs, review results afterward, and compare driving conditions. Vehicle-related measurements and heart rate data are brought together to provide a broader record of each drive.

## Recording a Session

ApexLogger is organized around logging individual track-day sessions and other performance-driving runs.

A normal session looks like this:

1. Begin logging before the drive starts.
2. Keep the Apple Watch active throughout the run.
3. Afterward, inspect values including GPS speed, boost, fuel pressure, timing corrections, and heart rate.
4. Compare the resulting records between runs or look for recurring patterns over time.

Keep the watch session and vehicle session synchronized as closely as possible so both represent the same driving period.
